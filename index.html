Specialized support

Training for women on GLP-1 medications

Rapid weight loss changes what your body needs from a workout. The mission is the same as it's always been — your program is adjusted for energy levels and built to protect strength and muscle while the medication does its part.

Strength-first programming

Resistance training prioritized to help preserve muscle mass during rapid weight change — not just generic cardio.

Sessions that flex with you

Intensity and volume adjusted week to week based on how you're feeling, energy dips included.
